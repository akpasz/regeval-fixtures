# Provenance

All fixture content in this repository is synthetic. Every customer, company,
person, transaction, alert, document, and memo is generated by the seeded
generator in `kyc-aml/generation/`, from invented-morphology vocabulary. No
fixture is derived from any real case, filing, or record.

## Provenance statement

Fixture construction was informed only by domain-author knowledge, public
regulatory or professional material, and explicitly synthetic design choices.
No proprietary implementation, customer data, confidential material, or
non-public CAIBots information informed fixture construction.

## Attribution rule

Every corpus-wide domain convention is attributable to one of: domain-author
input recorded in DOMAIN_REVIEW.md, public regulatory or professional
material, or an explicitly synthetic design choice recorded in
DESIGN_DECISIONS.md. No convention is justified solely by an appeal to how
banks generally operate.

## SAR boundary

This repository contains no Suspicious Activity Report content and simulates
no regulatory filing, filing confirmation, filing reference, or submission
event. Case notes and disposition memos represent internal investigative
material only. The distinction exists because SAR confidentiality rules make
even fictional filed-report imitations inappropriate in a public corpus.
