# Name screening procedure

Screening is a risk-reduction mechanism, not an assertion of legal or factual
uniqueness.

What the screen does: verifies that every person and company name appearing
in observable fixtures (1) exists in the generator's emitted name registry,
proving it was produced by the invented-morphology constructor rather than
typed by hand or drawn from a real-name pool, and (2) matches the morphology
pattern, whose syllable sets were chosen to avoid resemblance to common real
names in major naming traditions.

What the screen cannot do: prove that an invented string matches no real
person or entity anywhere. Global uniqueness is not established and is not
claimed. Residual risk is recorded in LIMITATIONS.md at Stage 6.

Execution metadata (names checked, findings, method version) is emitted to
the verification report at run time and never written into fixture content,
preserving determinism.
